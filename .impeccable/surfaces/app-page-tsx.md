Proof: CV-backed roles and dates, owned public repositories, concise case studies. Constraints: light theme, Inter, Lucide, accessible at narrow widths, no invented project outcomes or screenshots.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** A systems atlas introduces Joshua through the relationships he builds between interface, typed services, and cloud workflows. It refuses a generic portrait-and-badge opening.

**OWN-WORLD:** A cool mineral ground, deep teal ink, muted sea-glass panels, fine route lines, crisp rounded rectangles, and restrained annotation. Inter carries all text; Lucide marks controls. Diagram labels remain readable without color alone.

**STORY:** The first screen establishes Joshua's role and strengths. A conceptual flow shows his practice without pretending to document a specific client system. Selected work supplies three focused examples; experience, tools, and contact complete the route.
